Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and how we use them on Mt.Kadam Tours.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve performance, and gather basic information about how the site is used.

2. Cookies we use

We may use cookies to keep the website working properly, remember user choices, improve navigation, and understand broad traffic patterns. Some cookies are required for the site to function, while others help us improve the experience for future visitors.

3. Third-party cookies

We may allow trusted third-party services to place cookies on our website for analytics, advertising, or embedded content. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can usually manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect parts of the website or limit some features.

5. Changes

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or improvements to our services. Any updates will be published on this page.
