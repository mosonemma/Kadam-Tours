Day by day

Inside the Kidepo Safari Adventure

A five-day arc from Karamoja's plains to its wildest park.

DAY 01

Arrival & Karamoja Orientation

Pickup, transfer to Moroto, welcome briefing and an evening kraal visit.

DAY 02

Mount Moroto Trek

Guided ridge trek with panoramic views over the Karamoja plains.

DAY 03

Transfer to Kidepo Valley

Scenic drive north, afternoon game drive on arrival.

DAY 04

Full-Day Kidepo Safari

Sunrise and sunset game drives, Narus Valley and Kanangorok hot springs.

DAY 05

Departure

Final morning game drive, breakfast, and transfer back.
