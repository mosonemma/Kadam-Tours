Day by day

A taste of the 6-day Karamoja Cultural Exclusive Tour

Meet local communities, explore traditional and artisanal mining, and travel from Nakapiripirit through Moroto to Kotido.

DAY 01

Traditional village and boda-boda tour

Transfer to Nakapiripirit and explore a Karamojong traditional ere or village on a boda-boda cultural tour.

DAY 02

Mt. Kadam campsite hike

Hike to the campsite for scenic views. The supplied itinerary describes the route as moderate and suitable without previous mountain-climbing experience.

DAY 03

Cattle market and mining-site tour

Visit the cattle market during the day and take a boda-boda tour around Nakapiripirit's mining sites.

DAY 04

Artisanal gold mining and nature walk

Tour a village artisanal gold-mining site, then enjoy an evening nature walk around the district.

DAY 05

Moroto kraals, marble mining and Tepeth visit

Transfer to Moroto to visit traditional kraals and marble-mining areas, and meet Tepeth natives of Moroto.

DAY 06

Kotido, Nakapelimoru and return

Visit the largest village in East Africa and the sliding rock in Kotido, then depart for Kampala or Entebbe the same day.
