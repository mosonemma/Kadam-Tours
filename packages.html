Day by day

A taste of the 6-day Karamoja Exclusive Tour

From a warm community welcome to a final wildlife drive in Kidepo, each day brings a new side of Karamoja.

DAY 01

Meet Karamoja in Nakapiripirit

Transfer into town, then get a local perspective on everyday community life on a guided boda-boda tour.

DAY 02

Cattle market, Pian Upe & a kraal night

See the cattle market, head out for a game drive in Pian Upe, then settle in for a night under the stars and a kraal experience.

DAY 03

Waterfall trail near Moroto

Transfer to Moroto and stretch your legs on a hike to St. Lawrence waterfall.

DAY 04

History and onward to Kotido

Visit the Nawoikorot Idi Amin mass graves in Napak before continuing to Kotido.

DAY 05

Village life, sliding rock & Kidepo

Explore Nakapelimoru, described in the itinerary as East Africa's largest village, visit the sliding rock, then transfer to Kidepo.

DAY 06

Wild Kidepo

Take a game drive through Kidepo Valley National Park to close out your Karamoja adventure.
