7 Days Karamoja Adventure

Granite mountains, open plains, coffee slopes, community kraals and one of Uganda's wildest national parks — a week that moves from the Nile to the far north-east and back.

Book This Adventure Back to Packages
Duration7 Days
RouteKaramoja, Northeastern Uganda
ExperienceCultural & Adventure Safari
PriceFrom $400
The journey

One week, from the Nile to Kidepo

Start at the Source of the Nile, climb into the coffee country around Sipi Falls, then cross into Karamoja: Pian Upe's open wildlife plains, a hike on Mt. Kadam with a night in a community kraal, mining communities and a waterfall hike near Moroto, and finally the savannah of Kidepo Valley National Park.

Every stop is led by local guides, and community visits are arranged with the people who live there, so what you see is theirs to show.

Day by day

Your seven-day itinerary

Drive times are approximate and depend on road and weather conditions. Activities are confirmed with you before booking.

DAY 01

Source of the Nile & transfer to Sipi

Kampala → Jinja → Sipi
  • MorningPickup and drive from Kampala to Jinja (about 2–3 hours).
  • AfternoonVisit the Source of the Nile, where the river leaves Lake Victoria. Then drive on towards Mt. Elgon and Sipi, roughly 4–5 hours with stops.
  • EveningCheck in at Sipi, dinner and an early night.

Overnight: Sipi [lodge to be confirmed]

DAY 02

Sipi Falls, coffee & Pian Upe game drive

Sipi → Pian Upe → Nakapiripirit
  • MorningGuided nature walk past the Sipi waterfalls, followed by a coffee tour with a local farming family: picking, roasting and tasting.
  • AfternoonTransfer to Pian Upe Wildlife Reserve (about 3–4 hours) for a game drive across its open plains.
  • EveningDrive to Nakapiripirit town for dinner and rest.

Overnight: Nakapiripirit town [guesthouse to be confirmed]

DAY 03

Mt. Kadam hike & a night in the kraal

Nakapiripirit → Mt. Kadam
  • MorningTransfer to the foot of Mt. Kadam and meet your local hiking guides.
  • AfternoonHike up to the campsite and back, with views over the Karamoja plains. Pace is set by the group.
  • EveningDinner and a night in the kraal with the host community. Expect simple, basic accommodation.

Overnight: Community kraal, Mt. Kadam area

DAY 04

Mining communities & Moroto waterfall

Mt. Kadam → Moroto
  • MorningVisit a community mining town and meet the people who work there. Access is arranged with community leaders and may change.
  • AfternoonTransfer to Moroto and hike to the waterfall with a local guide.
  • EveningFree time in Moroto town and dinner.

Overnight: Moroto town [lodge to be confirmed]

DAY 05

Transfer to Kidepo & community tour

Moroto → Kidepo Valley
  • MorningEarly start for the long drive north to Kidepo Valley National Park (allow most of the day; exact time to be confirmed).
  • AfternoonCommunity tour near the park, meeting local residents and learning about their way of life.
  • EveningDinner and sunset at your camp or lodge.

Overnight: Kidepo area [camp or lodge to be confirmed]

DAY 06

Full-day Kidepo game drive

Kidepo Valley National Park
  • MorningSunrise game drive, when animals are most active.
  • AfternoonRest through the midday heat, then a second game drive across the savannah. Wildlife seen depends on the season and luck.
  • EveningCampfire or dinner at your accommodation.

Overnight: Kidepo area [camp or lodge to be confirmed]

DAY 07

Return to Kampala

Kidepo → Kampala
  • MorningEarly breakfast and a final short game drive if time allows, then depart.
  • AfternoonLong road transfer south. Expect a full day of driving, with stops for meals and stretching.
  • EveningArrive in Kampala and drop-off at the agreed point. Tour ends.

Tour ends in Kampala
