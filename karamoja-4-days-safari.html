4 Days Karamoja Safari

Waterfalls and coffee country, open wildlife plains, a community visit and a Kidepo game drive — a compact taste of Uganda's northeast.

The journey

From Sipi's green slopes to Kidepo's wild plains

Begin with Sipi Falls and a coffee tour, then travel into Karamoja for wildlife and a community cultural visit. Continue toward Kidepo, with a stop at Nakapelimoru, and finish your journey with a game drive before heading toward Kampala or Entebbe.

The itinerary below follows the four-day route provided. Your tour team will confirm the practical details, including departure timing and what is covered by your final quote.

Day by day

Your four-day itinerary

Travel times and activity timing depend on road and weather conditions.

DAY 01

Sipi Falls, coffee & sunset over Karamoja

Sipi → Karamoja plains
  • MorningExplore Sipi Falls and enjoy a local coffee tour.
  • EveningTake in the sunset views over the Karamoja plains.
DAY 02

Pian Upe wildlife & a village visit

Sipi → Pian Upe → Nakapiripirit
  • DayTransfer to Pian Upe Wildlife Reserve for a game drive, then visit a Karimojong village for a community cultural experience.
  • EveningRest in Nakapiripirit town.
DAY 03

Visit Nakapelimoru & continue to Kidepo

Karamoja → Kidepo Valley National Park
  • ExperienceTravel toward Kidepo National Park and visit Nakapelimoru, described in the supplied itinerary as East Africa's largest village.
DAY 04

Kidepo game drive & onward journey

Kidepo → Kampala / Entebbe
  • MorningTake a game drive in Kidepo Valley National Park.
  • DepartureLeave for Kampala or Entebbe after the safari.
