Murchison Falls: Boat Cruise or Game Drive First?

Safari Guide — January 2026

How to sequence your day so you catch the falls, the hippos, and the best light.

Murchison Falls is Uganda’s largest park, and it’s built for exactly this dilemma: savannah on one side, a churning stretch of the Nile on the other. The good news is you don’t have to choose — most travelers do both in a single day. The real question is which one goes first.

Why Morning Belongs to the Game Drive

Early morning and late afternoon are the prime windows for game drives — cooler temperatures mean animals are more active, and the light is better for photos too. Most operators start game drives around 6:00 AM, working the park’s northern sector, where the open savannah holds lions, elephants, giraffes, and antelope.

Go early and you avoid the midday heat that pushes wildlife into shade and stillness — by late morning, sightings thin out noticeably.

Why the Afternoon Belongs to the Boat

By the time the game drive wraps, it’s a natural point to head back to the lodge, have lunch, and rest before the afternoon cruise. The boat heads up the Victoria Nile toward the base of the falls, passing hippos, buffalo, elephants, and Nile crocodiles along the banks, plus a steady stream of birdlife — the boat’s advantage is getting close to animals calmly, without disturbing them, in a way a vehicle can’t.

Afternoon timing isn’t just about logistics — it’s about the payoff. The later cruise slot is often the most memorable, as dusk settles in and the light turns everything a warm orange. And the afternoon is also when animals congregate at the riverbanks to cool off, which means better sightings from the water.

Adding the Falls Themselves

If you want the full picture in one day, the classic move is to combine the boat cruise with the hike to the top of the falls — cruise up from Paraa, disembark near the base, then walk up for the view looking straight down into the gorge. That hike takes roughly 45 minutes to an hour, and it’s the one place you get to feel the spray and the noise before you ever see the falls from below.

A Simple Day Plan

  1. 6:00–9:30 AM — Game drive in the northern sector, cool air and active wildlife
  2. Midday — Back to the lodge, lunch, rest before the heat peaks
  3. Early-mid afternoon — Boat cruise up the Nile toward the falls
  4. Late afternoon — Disembark and hike to the top of the falls for sunset light

When to Visit

Boat cruises run year-round, but the dry months — December to February and June to October — are considered best, since animals gather more predictably along the riverbanks to drink.
