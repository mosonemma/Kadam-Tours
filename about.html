Our Story

Travel with purpose

Mt.Kadam Tours was founded to showcase the hidden treasures of Karamoja while creating real opportunities for the people who live there.

We believe tourism should go beyond sightseeing. Every journey should contribute to cultural preservation, economic growth, and community empowerment.

Through responsible tourism, we build meaningful experiences that connect travelers with authentic local traditions and landscapes most of Uganda has yet to discover.
