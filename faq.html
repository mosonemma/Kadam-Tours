Questions, answered

Everything travelers ask us before heading to Karamoja.

How do I book a tour?
Choose a package on our Packages page, or use the Booking page to select dates, group size, and add-ons. We confirm by email within 24 hours.
What's included in the package price?
Accommodation, ground transport, a professional guide, and listed activities. International flights, visas, and personal spending are not included unless stated.
What is your cancellation policy?
Full refund up to 14 days before departure, 50% refund within 7–14 days, and no refund inside 7 days. We can usually reschedule instead of cancelling.
How do I get to Karamoja?
Most travelers fly into Entebbe, then either drive (7–8 hours) or take a short charter flight to Moroto. We can arrange either as part of your package.
What's the best time of year to visit?
The dry seasons (December–March and June–August) offer the best wildlife viewing and easiest road conditions. Green season brings dramatic scenery and fewer crowds.
Do I need a visa to visit Uganda?
Most visitors need an e-visa, arranged online before arrival. We can send you the current requirements once you book.
Is Karamoja safe to visit?
Yes. Karamoja has become significantly safer over the past decade. We only operate on vetted routes and stay in close contact with local authorities and communities.
Do you provide travel insurance?
We require travelers to arrange their own travel insurance covering medical evacuation, and can recommend providers on request.
What should I pack?
Light, breathable layers for daytime, a warm layer for cool mountain nights, sturdy walking shoes, sun protection, and a reusable water bottle. We send a full packing list after booking.
Do I need special gear for mountain treks?
Sturdy hiking boots and trekking poles are recommended for Mount Kadam or Moroto. Technical climbing gear is not required for our standard routes.

Still have questions?

Reach out directly and we'll get back to you fast.
