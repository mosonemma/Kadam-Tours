Choose your climb

A mountain experience made for your schedule

Both options start with a hike from the mountain's base toward the campsite. The one-day route returns to the starting point on the same day. Choose the two-day experience to spend a night at camp, continue to the summit, and return to Nakapiripirit town for a rest.

Hiking pace, local guiding, camping arrangements and final departure details are coordinated with your group before travel.

Your options

One-day or two-day hike

Pick the itinerary that best suits your time and the kind of mountain day you want.

OPTION 01

One-day campsite hike

Mt. Kadam → campsite → return
  • StartMeet your local guides and begin the ascent toward the campsite.
  • On trailEnjoy the mountain landscape and take breaks as needed along the route.
  • ReturnHike back down to the starting point on the same day.
OPTION 02

Two-day campsite and summit hike

Day 1: campsite · Day 2: summit and return
  • Day 1Hike to the campsite and spend the night at camp.
  • Day 2Continue to the summit, then descend and return to Nakapiripirit town for a rest.
